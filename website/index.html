MIT TECH KERNEL

MIT TECH KERNEL is the technical club of MIT Mumbai, built around learning, experimentation, collaboration, and technology.

WHO WE ARE

A student-driven technical community where members explore different areas of computer science and emerging technology through projects, events, workshops, and collaborative learning.

WHAT WE DO

  • Build technical projects
  • Conduct workshops and technical sessions
  • Organize competitions and hackathons
  • Explore emerging technologies
  • Encourage peer-to-peer learning
  • Provide a platform for students to experiment and create
